★ How we work

Three unusual habits
that keep us making things.

exhibit ·003 ✿
🗑️ ★ Ritual #1

Friday kill list

Every Friday we delete the thing we're most afraid to cut. We've killed 14 features this way. Revenue went up. Nobody wrote in.

— ferdinand approves ✓ ✦
📮 ★ Ritual #2

Postcards, not press releases

Every customer who renewed gets a real paper postcard in the mail. Hand-written. Stamped. Sometimes decorated. Our retention is 97%. Correlation? Causation? Either way.

— 2,400 stamps and counting ✿
☕ ★ Ritual #3

Wednesday slow-down

No meetings before noon on Wednesdays. None. Ever. The mornings are for the deep work we promised each other we'd do. The afternoons are for the meetings we actually can't skip.

— sacred since 2023 ★

↑ none of these are in the employee handbook. all of them are in the culture.
