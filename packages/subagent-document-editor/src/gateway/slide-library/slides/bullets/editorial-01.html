Dispatches from the field.

Notebook · Five entries

Entry 01

The rewrite that wasn't.

We scoped a six-month rewrite in January and delivered none of it. Instead, the team shipped eleven small fixes that together accounted for the largest reliability improvement of the year.

Entry 02

A kinder sales motion.

We stopped chasing accounts that didn't want us. Pipeline shrank by a third. Win rate doubled. Sales cycles compressed to under a month.

Entry 03

The email we almost didn't send.

A plain-text newsletter written by a single engineer outperformed our five-person marketing calendar three weeks running.

Entry 04

The meeting we killed.

Removing the Tuesday strategy sync returned sixteen hours a week to the org. No one asked to bring it back.

Entry 05

Trust as a product feature.

Publishing our incident post-mortems in full moved our NPS more than any onboarding change we shipped. Customers want to see the work, not the polish.
