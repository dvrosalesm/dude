Chronologie de la Maison

One hundred & forty years, in five moments.

A ledger of the hours that mattered most to an atelier that has, as it always has, made no particular haste about any of them.

1886 An I The first bench. Léon Aurelia opens a single workshop on the rue de Turenne with two benches and an apprentice.
1924 An XXXVIII The first exhibition. A piece by the maison is acquired for the collection of the Musée des Arts Décoratifs.
1968 An LXXXII The long winter. The atelier closes to outside orders for five years. The house uses the time to train seven apprentices.
2002 An CXVI The fifth generation. Hélène Aurelia-Sorel takes the desk her great-great-grandfather occupied. The ledger does not change hands.
2026 An CXL The present hour. Seven benches. Twelve makers. One ledger — now in its 43rd volume, kept in longhand.
