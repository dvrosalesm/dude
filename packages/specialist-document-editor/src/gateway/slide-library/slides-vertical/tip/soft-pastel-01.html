Ritual iiiFor the bath-hour

A bath, drawn
with intention.

Pour the water slowly. Light one candle — no more. Add a spoonful of honey and a sprig of rosemary from the kitchen. No phone, no music. This hour is nobody's but yours.

— You'll need

honey · rosemary · one cotton cloth · a candle that smells of something nearly forgotten
