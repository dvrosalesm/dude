five slow
things to do on sundays

for the days when nothing is urgent. a list kept in the kitchen notebook since spring 2024.

i.

Bake something that takes all afternoon

A sourdough if you have the flour. A pot of beans if you don't. It's about the hours, not the dish.

ii.

Write one short letter

On paper — to someone you haven't spoken to since last year. Don't edit it. Just mail it.

iii.

Cut flowers from your own garden

Or a neighbour's, with permission. Put them in the kitchen. Change the water before bed.

iv.

Walk the long way home

The one that passes the small park and the house with the blue door. No podcast. No headphones.

v.

Re-read the book of the season

The one that's always on the nightstand. Start from any page; it'll find where you need to be.
