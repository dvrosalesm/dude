Founded

Three engineers in a Lisbon co-working space with one customer and a weekend to prove the idea could hold weight.

The first rewrite

The only time we've ever thrown away the core. It cost a year. It bought the next five.

The pivot that wasn't

We almost sold to become a plug-in. The deal fell apart on a comma. We kept the company. A kindness, in hindsight.

2023

The first profitable month

A small one — $28K of profit — but the team printed the bank statement and pinned it above the coffee machine.

2026

Today

Seventy-three people. Eleven countries. One archive that has, improbably, outlived every rewrite we planned for it.
