The archivist's year.

A conversation with Iris Oduya on tending old systems, slow wins, and why the boring work wins decades.

Photograph by M. Adebayo · March 2026

The interview

Iris has spent eleven years inside the same archive system. Four managers, three rewrites, two acquisitions. The code she touched in her first week is still in production. Ask her about it and she'll pretend it's not a compliment.

"The fashion is always some new framework," she said over coffee in January. "The work is always the same. You read. You listen. You fix the thing nobody else wants to read."

Her team shipped a 77% reduction in retrieval time without adding a single new service.
