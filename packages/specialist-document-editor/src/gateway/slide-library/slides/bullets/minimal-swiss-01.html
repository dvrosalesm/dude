Six things we learned this quarter. Field Notes · 003
  1. Customer acquisition costs rose 18%.
    Paid channels softened in late February. Organic pickup partially offset the gap.
  2. Retention is our most durable lever.
    Cohorts from Q3 are still compounding revenue at 9% month over month.
  3. Pricing held firm in the mid-tier.
    No discount pressure across 142 renewals. Discounted deals converted 12% worse.
  4. Self-serve outpaced sales-led.
    Self-serve crossed 54% of new logos for the first time since 2024.
  5. Support volume dropped 22%.
    Shipping the new onboarding in January removed the top three ticket types.
  6. Engineering velocity normalized.
    Cycle time returned to pre-rewrite baseline. Reliability improved in parallel.
