Key findings from current-state diagnostic

Exhibit 2 · Page 14 of 42
In summary — the organization's complexity is self-inflicted and addressable. Six observations recur across interviews, data, and process mapping. Three are structural and require board-level decisions; three are operational and can be addressed within the line.
01

Duplicated finance functions across four regions

Each region maintains its own close, AP, and reporting team. Benchmarks suggest a 40% reduction is achievable without impact to control environment.

Structural
02

Procurement leverage sits below peer benchmarks

Spend of $312M is fragmented across 2,400 vendors; top-100 vendors account for 43% of spend versus peer median of 61%.

Structural
03

Technology landscape is layered, not integrated

Fourteen overlapping systems in finance alone, each with active licenses; consolidation could release $6M in software spend over three years.

Structural
04

Order-to-cash cycle longer than peer median

DSO of 54 days versus peer median of 41; majority of the gap is in dispute resolution, not collections. Addressable in 90 days.

Operational
05

Decision rights unclear below EVP level

Interviews cite average decision latency of 19 days for cross-functional trade-offs. A RACI refresh would compress this materially.

Operational
06

Performance metrics are lagging, not leading

Board pack is 78% financial and retrospective; we propose adding four leading indicators covering pipeline, service, and people.
