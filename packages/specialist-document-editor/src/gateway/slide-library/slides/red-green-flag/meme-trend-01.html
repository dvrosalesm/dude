red flags vs green flags
in a tech job interview.

Meme format · red/green flag
🚩 run away fast
  • ✕
    "We work hard and play hard"→ translation: play hard = 7pm lunch, work hard = 11pm deploy
  • ✕
    "We don't really have titles"a choice→ so also no leveling, ladder, or raises
  • ✕
    Recruiter "personally loved your github"→ your github is three forks of create-next-app
  • ✕
    The office has a slide in the lobby→ the slide will not save you from the outage tonight
  • ✕
    "We're a family"→ families do not have performance reviews
★ score · 3+ = no thanks
🟢 sign today
  • ✓
    Published comp bands and they stick to themrare→ you don't have to negotiate your own dignity
  • ✓
    Clear on-call rotation with opt-in bonus→ or better, no on-call in the first 6 months
  • ✓
    The interviewer has been there > 3 years→ cannot fake this; retention is a signal
  • ✓
    "Here's the doc that got killed last month"→ they publish the misses, not just the wins
  • ✓
    Asked "what's your boundary on hours?"→ unprompted. this is the one.
★ score · 3+ = you're home
