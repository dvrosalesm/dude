## Palette
- `--bg`: #f1e4c1 (vellum parchment)
- `--ink`: #2a1a0a (oak gall ink)
- `--accent`: #9c2a2a (vermillion rubric) or #1c3a6e (lapis)
- `--gold`: #c9a961 (illuminated gold leaf)
- `--muted`: #7a6a4a

## Typography
- Headlines: UnifrakturCook, Cloister Black, or a blackletter — uppercase rare, use drop caps heavily
- Body: EB Garamond, Cormorant, or a humanist serif, 400 weight, generous line-height (1.6)
- First letter of paragraphs often set as an oversized illuminated initial, 4–6 lines tall, in gold on a vermillion square.

## Composition
- Single or two-column manuscript layout. Justified text with hyphenation.
- Marginalia: small illustrations, pointing hands (manicules), tiny vines curling down the margins.
- Rubrication: section headers in red ink, inline with text.
- Heavy borders: floral vines, interlaced knotwork (Celtic/Insular style) around the page.

## Textures & details
- Subtle parchment noise/stain at 10–15% opacity.
- Gold leaf rendered with a slight shimmer gradient (#c9a961 → #e8c67a).
- Hand-drawn ornamental capitals and drop caps.

## Avoid
- Sans-serif type, clean digital gradients, modern color palettes, grid-based minimalism, photographs.
