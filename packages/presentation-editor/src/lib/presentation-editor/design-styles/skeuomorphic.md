## Palette
- `--bg`: #e8e2d6 (linen/paper) or #2d2418 (stitched leather)
- `--surface`: #f7f2e8 (paper surface)
- `--accent`: #4a6fa5 (ink blue) or #8b5a3c (leather tan)
- `--text`: #1a1208
- `--muted`: #6a5a44

## Typography
- Headlines: Caslon, Baskerville, or a humanist serif — 700 weight, moderate tracking
- Body: Georgia or Source Serif Pro, 400 weight
- Optional handwritten accents (Caveat, Homemade Apple) for annotations.

## Composition
- Real-world object metaphors: slides look like pages of a leather-bound journal, index cards, Polaroids, or a wooden desk.
- Layered physical elements: paper cards, paperclips, tape strips, sticky notes.
- Stitched leather edges, stitch marks, embossed logos.
- Content is "placed" on the surface — not abstract.

## Textures & details
- Subtle paper grain, canvas weave, or leather pebble texture at 15–25% opacity.
- Soft inner shadows on recessed elements; soft outer shadows on lifted elements.
- Beveled edges, subtle highlights on top of raised elements.
- Realistic material cues: paper curl at corners, stitch dashes, washi tape.

## Avoid
- Flat design, pure digital gradients without material cues, neon colors, abstract minimalism, sans-serif body.
