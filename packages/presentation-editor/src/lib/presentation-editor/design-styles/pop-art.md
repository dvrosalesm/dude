## Palette
- `--bg`: #fff8e1 (comic cream) or pure white
- Primaries: #ff1744 (pop red), #ffd600 (pop yellow), #2962ff (pop blue), #000000
- `--text`: #000000

## Typography
- Headlines: Bangers, Luckiest Guy, or Anton — comic-display fonts, uppercase, bold, tight tracking
- Body: Inter Bold or Futura Bold — no thin weights
- BIG SPEECH BUBBLES with words like "POW!", "BAM!", "NEW!" (use sparingly).

## Composition
- Flat primary-color panels divided by 3–5px black outlines.
- Comic panels: split the slide into 2–4 cells with thick black gutters.
- Halftone dot patterns (Ben-Day dots) as backgrounds or shadows.
- Onomatopoeic callouts and starbursts to highlight key stats.

## Textures & details
- Ben-Day halftone dots (repeating radial-gradient), especially on photos.
- Thick 3–5px black outlines everywhere (à la Lichtenstein).
- Speech bubbles and thought clouds as callouts.
- Starburst shapes with "NEW!" / "WOW!" labels.

## Avoid
- Pastels, soft gradients, serif body, photo-realistic imagery, minimalism, muted earth tones.
