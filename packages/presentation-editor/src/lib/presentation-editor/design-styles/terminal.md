## Palette
- `--bg`: #0a0e0a (CRT black) or #1a1a1a
- `--text`: #3bfe7e (phosphor green) — or choose amber #ffbf00, or white #e6e6e6
- `--accent`: #3bfe7e
- `--muted`: rgba(59,254,126,0.45)

## Typography
- Everything: IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono, or "VT323" — one family only, one weight
- Uppercase for headlines, no proportional spacing changes.
- Use a blinking caret `█` at end of active lines.

## Composition
- Emulate a terminal window: optional window chrome with `[user@host ~]$` prompts.
- Content as ASCII tables, boxed in `+---+` characters.
- Left-aligned, monospaced columns. Everything on a strict character grid.
- Section headers as `## INDEX ##` or `> SECTION 01`.

## Textures & details
- CRT scanlines (1–2px) at 6–10% opacity.
- Subtle screen curvature/vignette.
- Occasional "typing" animation for demos (not required).
- ASCII art dividers: `═══════` or `- - - - -`.

## Avoid
- Proportional fonts, color beyond 2–3 phosphor values, gradients, images, rounded corners, drop shadows.
