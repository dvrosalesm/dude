## Palette
- `--bg`: #e8e4dc (raw paper) or #111111 (concrete)
- `--accent`: #ff3b00 (construction orange) or #ffe600 (hazard yellow)
- `--text`: #000000
- `--muted`: #555555

## Typography
- Headlines: Archivo Black, Helvetica Bold, or a mono display — ALL CAPS, tight tracking, heavy weight
- Body: IBM Plex Mono or Space Mono, 500 weight
- Use type at brutal scale: headlines 12–20cqmin. No elegance.

## Composition
- Exposed structure: visible rules, thick `border: 4px solid var(--text)` on blocks.
- Asymmetric, offset grids. Things butt against edges of the slide, no generous margins.
- Left-align everything. Hard corners. No border-radius.
- Show the scaffolding — label sections with `## 01 / TITLE` style numbering.

## Textures & details
- Thick black dividers. Boxed captions. Monospace timestamps.
- Occasional paper grain or concrete noise overlay at 5–10% opacity.
- Crosshatch fills instead of solid fills when accenting.

## Avoid
- Gradients, soft shadows, rounded corners, serif body type, pastel colors, centered layouts.
