## Palette
- `--bg`: vibrant gradient background — e.g., `radial-gradient(at 30% 20%, #ff77e9, #7b5cff 50%, #0ea5e9)`
- `--glass`: rgba(255,255,255,0.12) with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`
- `--stroke`: rgba(255,255,255,0.25) (1px inner border)
- `--text`: #ffffff
- `--muted`: rgba(255,255,255,0.7)

## Typography
- Headlines: Inter Display, SF Pro Display, or similar modern sans — 600 weight, moderate tracking
- Body: Inter or SF Pro Text, 400 weight, 1.5 line-height
- White or near-white type on blurred glass cards.

## Composition
- Vibrant, slightly out-of-focus background (gradient mesh, blurred photo, or abstract blobs).
- Primary content lives inside translucent glass cards with rounded corners (20–32px).
- Soft drop shadows beneath cards for lift.
- Layered depth: multiple glass cards at different z-levels, overlapping gently.

## Textures & details
- Thin 1px white stroke on glass edges (top-left highlight).
- Subtle inner glow on cards.
- Blurred color blobs behind glass to create color bleed.
- Light noise (1–2% opacity) over the blur to prevent banding.

## Avoid
- Flat opaque backgrounds, hard edges, monochrome palettes, serif body type, brutalist rules, dense text.
