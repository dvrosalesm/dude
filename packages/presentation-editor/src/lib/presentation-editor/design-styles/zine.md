## Palette
- `--bg`: #f2ece0 (photocopy paper) — stays mostly monochrome
- `--text`: #111111 (photocopy black)
- `--accent`: #e63946 (hand-stamped red) or highlighter #fff47a — used for emphasis
- `--muted`: #6a6a6a

## Typography
- Mix: a typewriter font (Courier Prime, American Typewriter), a chunky cut-from-magazine headline (Archivo Black scaled huge), and handwritten annotations (Caveat, Shadows Into Light)
- Glue-collage aesthetic: letters cut from different fonts to spell words.
- Crooked baselines, offset by 1–3° rotations.

## Composition
- Cut-paste collage: rectangles of paper glued at rough angles, headlines torn from newspapers.
- Photocopy-degraded imagery: high-contrast black-and-white, blown-out, streaky.
- Stapled or taped seams visible.
- Margin scribbles, arrows drawn by hand pointing to important words.

## Textures & details
- Photocopy streaks, toner smudges, fingerprints.
- Staple marks at the binding edge.
- Handwritten corrections, underlines, circles, cross-outs.
- Stamp marks ("URGENT", "DIY") and highlighter streaks.

## Avoid
- Clean vector graphics, saturated color, neat alignment, drop shadows, modern sans-serif body in perfect kerning, corporate polish.
