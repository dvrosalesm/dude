## Palette
- `--bg`: #f4f1ec (warm linen white)
- `--accent`: #8a7a62 (oatmeal) or #3a5a40 (forest) — muted, nature-derived
- `--text`: #2a2a2a
- `--muted`: #8a8a8a

## Typography
- Headlines: Söhne, Inter, or Graphik — 500 weight, moderate tracking
- Body: Inter or IBM Plex Sans, 400 weight, 1.6 line-height
- Titles set calmly — no shouting. Lowercase headlines feel natural.

## Composition
- Heavy negative space. Content clusters to one side of the slide.
- Subtle asymmetric balance with a hero image or single graphic element.
- Left-aligned body, generous margins.
- Section breaks with single thin rules or just whitespace.

## Textures & details
- Natural material photography: wood, wool, stone, plants.
- Flat color blocks in muted earth tones.
- Hairline rules only. No heavy ornament.
- Quiet, functional iconography when icons appear.

## Avoid
- Saturated colors, neon, ornament, heavy borders, serif body, gradients, clutter, centered compositions.
