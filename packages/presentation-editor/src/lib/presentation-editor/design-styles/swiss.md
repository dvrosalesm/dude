## Palette
- `--bg`: #ffffff
- `--accent`: #d7263d (Swiss red) or #000000
- `--text`: #111111
- `--muted`: #6b6b6b

## Typography
- Headlines: Helvetica Neue or Inter, 700 weight, tight tracking (-0.02em)
- Body: Helvetica Neue or Inter, 400 weight, 1.4 line-height
- Strict type scale: one headline size, one body size, one caption size. No italics.

## Composition
- Objective, grid-driven: 12-column baseline, consistent gutters.
- Left-aligned body, flush-left ragged-right. Never center body text.
- Generous negative space. Content sits in the top 2/3 of the slide.
- Clear horizontal rules (1–2px) separating sections.

## Textures & details
- Small meta labels in ALL CAPS with 0.15em letter-spacing.
- Single bold color block or photo aligned to the grid — no decoration.
- Page numbers and section tags in the bottom margin.

## Avoid
- Drop shadows, gradients, decorative borders, multiple type families, serif fonts, emoji, clutter.
