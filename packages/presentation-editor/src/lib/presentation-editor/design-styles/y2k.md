## Palette
- `--bg`: linear-gradient(135deg, #c7d7ff, #ffe0f0) or #f0f4ff (frost white)
- `--chrome`: linear-gradient(180deg, #e8ecf5, #9eaac7, #e8ecf5)
- `--accent`: #7b5cff (electric lavender), #ff77e9 (bubblegum), #5eead4 (aqua)
- `--text`: #10123a (deep navy)

## Typography
- Headlines: Eurostile, Microgramma, or Orbitron — bubbly, rounded, often chrome-filled
- Body: Verdana, Tahoma, or SF Pro Rounded — rounded sans
- Titles often glossy, with inner highlight and outer bevel.

## Composition
- Frosted-glass cards with rounded corners (16–24px) and subtle inner highlight.
- Iridescent/holographic gradients on accents.
- Overlapping bubble shapes, floating orbs, lens flares.
- Chrome typography treatments with reflections.

## Textures & details
- Metallic gradients and gloss highlights.
- Bevelled buttons with 3D depth (but subtle, not skeuomorphic).
- Star sparkles, motion blur streaks, holographic rainbow edges.
- Low-saturation photography tinted cool.

## Avoid
- Flat design, matte colors, harsh grids, grunge textures, serif body, desaturated grays.
