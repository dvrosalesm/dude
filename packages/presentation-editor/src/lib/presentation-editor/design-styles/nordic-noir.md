## Palette
- `--bg`: #0f1214 (night) or #e6e7e9 (frost)
- `--accent`: #d97a1e (amber streetlight) or #8a2332 (blood red) — one accent only
- `--text`: #dcdce0 on dark, #0f1214 on light
- `--muted`: #5a6268

## Typography
- Headlines: a condensed grotesque (Oswald, Barlow Condensed, or Inter Tight) — 600 weight, wide tracking for small caps, tight for large
- Body: Inter or Söhne, 400 weight, 1.6 line-height, desaturated color
- Headlines often set quite small relative to the vast negative space around them.

## Composition
- Heavy negative space. Content occupies a small corner; the rest breathes cold and empty.
- One accent point of color in an otherwise desaturated field.
- Wide cinematic crops of landscape photography (fog, forest, coastline) in monochrome or duotone.
- Typography bottom-aligned, like a film title card.

## Textures & details
- Subtle film grain (2–5% noise).
- Duotone imagery using `--bg` and `--accent`.
- Thin hairline rules (0.5–1px), often just one per slide.
- Small meta labels (location, coordinates, timestamp) for atmosphere.

## Avoid
- Multiple accent colors, bright saturated palettes, cheerful imagery, ornamental type, dense grids, warmth.
