## Palette
- `--bg`: #f4ecd8 (aged paper)
- `--accent`: #6b8e23 (moss) or #8b5a3c (terracotta)
- `--secondary`: #c9a961 (muted gold)
- `--text`: #3a2c1a (dark umber)
- `--muted`: #8a7a5c

## Typography
- Headlines: Arnold Boecklin, Clicker Script, or a stylized display serif with organic curves
- Body: EB Garamond or Cormorant, 400 italic for flavor sentences
- Titles flow, often curved along a path or with decorative drop caps.

## Composition
- Organic, curvilinear layouts — content follows sweeping arcs rather than grids.
- Botanical borders: ivy, lilies, wisteria traced in thin lines around content.
- Asymmetric balance: heavy ornament top-left, open space bottom-right.
- Illustrated female figures or peacock motifs as anchor elements (use SVG or illustration).

## Textures & details
- Thin sinuous linework, 1–1.5px, in muted gold or brown.
- Flat muted jewel-tone color blocks.
- Vignettes and oval photo frames.

## Avoid
- Geometric grids, heavy black rules, sans-serif type, neon color, crisp digital edges, minimalism.
