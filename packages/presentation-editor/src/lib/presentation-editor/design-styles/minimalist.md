## Palette
- `--bg`: #ffffff or #fafafa
- `--accent`: one single color (choose: #111111, or a muted #c94d3e)
- `--text`: #111111
- `--muted`: #8a8a8a

## Typography
- Headlines: Inter, Söhne, or Helvetica Neue — 500 or 600 weight, tight tracking (-0.02em)
- Body: Inter or Söhne, 400 weight, 1.5 line-height
- One type family, two weights max. No decorative fonts.

## Composition
- Extreme negative space — content occupies 40–60% of the slide at most.
- Left-aligned or centered single focal point. Clear hierarchy.
- Thin hairline rules (1px) if needed. Often no rules at all.
- One idea per slide, stated in the fewest possible words.

## Textures & details
- No textures. No gradients. Flat color only.
- Occasional photograph — desaturated, one strong image per slide if used.
- Small metadata in muted gray (page number, date).

## Avoid
- Ornament, multiple accent colors, shadows, borders, patterns, decorative type, busy layouts, clip-art icons.
