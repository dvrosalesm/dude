## Palette
- `--bg`: linear-gradient(180deg, #1a0933 0%, #ff2a6d 50%, #ff9a6d 100%) or solid #0b0223
- `--neon-pink`: #ff2a6d, `--neon-cyan`: #5eead4, `--neon-purple`: #b14aff
- `--text`: #fff6ff
- `--muted`: #8a6eb0

## Typography
- Headlines: a heavy grotesque or "VCR OSD Mono", "Rubik Mono One" — uppercase, wide tracking, often with chromatic aberration
- Body: IBM Plex Mono, 400 weight
- Occasional Japanese hiragana/katakana accent characters (タダ, 波) for flavor.

## Composition
- Sunset gradient backdrops with a pink/orange horizon.
- Grid floor receding to a horizon line (vaporwave staple).
- Floating classical busts or Greek columns (silhouettes), dolphins, palm trees, chrome spheres.
- Asymmetric layouts with elements floating in 3D space.

## Textures & details
- VHS scanlines (2px repeating) and subtle chromatic aberration.
- 80s-90s glitch: text displacement, RGB split.
- Lens flares and lo-fi JPG compression artifacts (subtle).
- Chrome-gradient text fills.

## Avoid
- Corporate minimalism, serif bodies, warm natural photography, muted palettes, gridless layouts.
