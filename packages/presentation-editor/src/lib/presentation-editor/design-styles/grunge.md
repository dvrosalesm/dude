## Palette
- `--bg`: #1a1612 (charred) or #d9cfa6 (aged paper) with heavy texture
- `--accent`: #a83e1f (rust red) or #4a7d3e (army green)
- `--text`: #e8e0cc on dark, #1a1612 on light
- `--muted`: #6a6055

## Typography
- Headlines: Stencil, Trade Gothic Condensed Bold, or a weathered display — uppercase, aggressive tracking
- Body: Courier Prime or American Typewriter, 400 weight
- Type distressed with scratches, misregistration, offset.

## Composition
- Torn paper edges and taped layers.
- Photocopied, Xeroxed feel — high-contrast, blown-out imagery.
- Hand-scrawled annotations and cross-outs on top of neat type.
- Off-kilter rotations (-1° to -3°) on elements for imperfect feel.

## Textures & details
- Heavy paper grain, coffee stains, smudges.
- Spray paint stencils and splatter overlays.
- Strips of gaffer tape, torn labels, photocopy streaks.
- Cracked ink and misregistered color layers (offset 1–2px).

## Avoid
- Clean digital gradients, rounded corners, pastel palettes, perfect alignment, serif body, whitespace elegance.
