## Palette
- `--bg`: linear-gradient(180deg, #ff6b9d 0%, #ffa45b 55%, #ffd36b 100%) (Miami sunset)
- `--neon-pink`: #ff2d95, `--neon-cyan`: #3ae7ff, `--palm-teal`: #2ab6a7
- `--chrome`: linear-gradient(180deg, #fffaf0, #e0b870)
- `--text`: #1a0a2a or #ffffff

## Typography
- Headlines: Monoton, Audiowide, or an outlined display — neon sign look, thick strokes with outer glow
- Body: Inter or Futura, 500 weight
- Optional italic script secondary ("Miami", "Vice") for flair.

## Composition
- Sunset gradient backdrops (pink → orange → yellow).
- Palm tree silhouettes, flamingos, or Art Deco waves at the bottom.
- Chrome-filled typography with pink/cyan neon glow.
- Art Deco hotel flourishes: stepped rectangles, curved corners, pastel stripes.

## Textures & details
- Neon outer glow on headlines (`filter: drop-shadow(0 0 12px var(--neon-pink))`).
- Chrome gradient fills and bevels on main type.
- Grid horizon receding to sunset (nod to vaporwave but warmer).
- Subtle film grain.

## Avoid
- Cold minimalism, matte flat design, serif elegance, muted earth tones, monochrome black-and-white.
