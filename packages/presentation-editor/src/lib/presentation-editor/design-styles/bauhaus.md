## Palette
- `--bg`: #f2ede4 (warm off-white)
- `--primary`: #d62828 (red), `--secondary`: #1d3557 (blue), `--tertiary`: #ffd166 (yellow)
- `--text`: #111111
- `--muted`: #555555

## Typography
- Headlines: Futura, Montserrat, or a geometric sans — heavy weight, tight tracking, sometimes lowercase
- Body: Futura Book or Inter, 400 weight
- Occasional huge single initial letter used as a graphic element.

## Composition
- Primary shapes (circle, triangle, square) used structurally — not as decoration, but as layout anchors.
- Divide the slide into bold color fields: e.g., left third red, center yellow, right square blue.
- Strong diagonals and right angles. Compositions feel like a painting by Kandinsky.
- One slide = one strong geometric idea.

## Textures & details
- Flat fills, no gradients. Crisp edges.
- Occasional thin black outline (1–2px) around color fields.
- Numbers and captions set in small caps.

## Avoid
- Ornament, 3D effects, photographs (prefer abstract shapes), serif fonts, pastels.
