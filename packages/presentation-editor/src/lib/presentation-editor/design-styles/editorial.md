## Palette
- `--bg`: #faf7f2 (warm off-white)
- `--accent`: #b91c3c (editorial red) or #2a2a2a
- `--text`: #1a1a1a
- `--muted`: #6a6a6a

## Typography
- Headlines: Playfair Display, GT Sectra, or Tiempos — serif, 700 weight, elegant contrast
- Body: Source Serif Pro, Lora, or Georgia, 400 weight, 1.55 line-height
- Pull quotes: oversized serif italic, hanging quotation marks, centered.
- Drop caps on opening paragraphs (4-line height).

## Composition
- Magazine-spread feel: generous margins, classic 2-column body with a wide sidebar.
- Headlines set large and airy with subtle kerning.
- Imagery treated as hero photography with captions in small italic.
- Section breaks marked by a single ornament (❧) or a fine horizontal rule with center dingbat.

## Textures & details
- Subtle paper warmth (cream, not pure white).
- Italic captions in muted color.
- Bylines and section tags in small caps with letter-spacing.
- Hanging punctuation for refined typography.

## Avoid
- Sans-serif body type, neon colors, dense grids, brutalist rules, multiple accent colors, clip art.
