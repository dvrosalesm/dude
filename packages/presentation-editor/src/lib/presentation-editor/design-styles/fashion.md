## Palette
- `--bg`: #f5f2ed (bone/ivory) or #0a0a0a (couture black)
- `--accent`: #b8a994 (stone) or a single jewel tone (#7a1f1f burgundy)
- `--text`: #111111 on light, #f5f2ed on dark
- `--muted`: #8a8a8a

## Typography
- Headlines: Didot, Bodoni 72, or GT Sectra Display — extremely high-contrast serif, thin hairlines, massive scale (15–25cqmin)
- Body: Inter or Helvetica, 300–400 weight, wide tracking (0.05em)
- Wordmarks and captions in small caps with 0.2em tracking.

## Composition
- Full-bleed hero photography (model on plain backdrop) with type laid over or adjacent.
- Massive single-word titles: "SPRING", "EDITION 07" — set huge, centered or flush-left.
- Plenty of negative space. Compositions feel like a magazine cover.
- Issue numbers, edition markers, season tags in the corners.

## Textures & details
- Hairline rules (0.5–1px).
- Monochromatic or duotone photo treatment.
- Minimal UI chrome — let typography and imagery carry the slide.
- Elegant numerical sequences (01 / 07) instead of page numbers.

## Avoid
- Multiple saturated colors, sans-serif display type at small sizes, clip art, grunge, clutter, cheerful/casual tone.
