## Palette
- `--bg`: #0a0a0f (deep midnight) or #f7f1e3 (ivory)
- `--gold`: #c9a961, `--accent`: #1b2a4a (lacquer blue)
- `--text`: #f1e8d4 on dark, #1b1b1b on light
- `--muted`: rgba(201,169,97,0.5)

## Typography
- Headlines: Poiret One, Cinzel, or Limelight — high-contrast capitals, wide tracking (0.1em+)
- Body: Cormorant Garamond or Libre Baskerville, 400 weight
- Occasional ligatures and stylistic alternates. Centered titles work well.

## Composition
- Strict bilateral symmetry. Center compositions flanked by stepped geometric ornament.
- Stepped pyramids, sunbursts, chevrons, fan shapes as framing devices.
- Gold linework (1–2px) defining panels and divisions.
- Layered frames: outer border, inner border, content.

## Textures & details
- Thin gold rules with diamond or chevron terminators.
- Sunburst radials behind titles.
- Vertical side ornaments resembling elevator doors.

## Avoid
- Sans-serif body type, asymmetric grids, saturated primary colors, casual photography, modern iconography.
