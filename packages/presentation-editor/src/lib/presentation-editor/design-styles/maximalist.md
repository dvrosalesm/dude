## Palette
- `--bg`: deep pattern or clashing color — e.g., #2a1b3d with floral overlay, or #ff3860 with chartreuse accents
- Accents: mix 4–6 saturated colors: #ff3860, #ffd166, #06d6a0, #118ab2, #7b2cbf, #f4a261
- `--text`: chosen for contrast per section (#ffffff or #1a1a1a)

## Typography
- Headlines: mix wildly — a bold display serif (Playfair) next to a chunky sans (Archivo Black) next to a script. Use multiple type families per slide.
- Body: bold and full of character — no neutrals.
- Type stacked at varied sizes, colored differently word-by-word.

## Composition
- Layered everything: pattern on pattern, image on pattern, type on image.
- Asymmetric, collage-like. Stickers, ornaments, badges stacked.
- Edges bleed; no safe whitespace. Every inch earns its place.
- Multiple focal points held together by repeated color.

## Textures & details
- Floral/toile patterns, leopard prints, damask, stripes — mixed together.
- Badges, seals, ribbons, stickers as decorative overlays.
- Thick outlines around elements in contrasting colors.
- Halftone accents, confetti, sparkles.

## Avoid
- Whitespace-heavy minimalism, monochrome palettes, serif-only layouts, quiet restraint.
