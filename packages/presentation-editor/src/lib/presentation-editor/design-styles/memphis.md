## Palette
- `--bg`: #fdf6e3 (cream) or #ffffff
- Accents: #ff3860 (hot pink), #00c4cc (teal), #ffd166 (yellow), #2d2d2d (black)
- `--text`: #1a1a1a

## Typography
- Headlines: Monotype Corsiva replaced with something chunky — use "Druk", "Archivo Black", or a playful display — mix cases freely
- Body: Inter or Futura, 500 weight
- Title words can be different colors; baseline shift for playfulness.

## Composition
- Collage energy: scatter shapes (squiggles, triangles, half-circles, confetti) behind and around content.
- Patterns: black-and-white stripes, grids of dots, checkerboards, zigzag — used as color blocks.
- Overlap shapes and photos at playful angles. Nothing sits perfectly horizontal.
- Bright color blocks butt against patterned blocks.

## Textures & details
- Squiggle lines, starburst shapes, thick diagonal stripes.
- 2–3px black outlines around key elements.
- Small random polka-dot fields as accents.

## Avoid
- Monochrome palettes, minimalism, serif type, drop shadows, realistic photography without treatment.
