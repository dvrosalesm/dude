## Palette
- `--bg`: #f3ead5 (aged cream) or #2a1f16 (mahogany)
- `--accent`: #7a1f1f (oxblood) or #2f4f2f (hunter green)
- `--gold`: #b58a3a
- `--text`: #1a1208
- `--muted`: #6a5a44

## Typography
- Headlines: Playfair Display Black, IM Fell DW Pica, or a slab/didone with extreme stroke contrast — mix sizes aggressively, wooden-type poster feel
- Body: Libre Caslon or Cormorant, 400 weight
- Embrace display type variety: small caps, ligatures, swashes, multiple weights stacked.

## Composition
- Dense, centered, symmetrical handbill compositions.
- Stacked headlines of wildly different sizes — a broadside or music-hall poster.
- Ornate frames: scrollwork, filigree, cartouches around content blocks.
- Multiple horizontal rules (double lines, ornamented dividers) between sections.

## Textures & details
- Engraved illustrations or stippled portraits (sepia-toned).
- Floral flourishes and ornamental brackets (❦ ❧ ✤) as dividers.
- Slight paper-grain noise, faded corners.

## Avoid
- Minimalism, sans-serif type, modern flat color, clean grids, neon palettes, centered body text at modern sizes.
