## Palette
- `--bg`: #e8dcc4 (Florentine plaster)
- `--accent`: #8b2635 (cardinal red) or #2c5282 (Tuscan blue)
- `--gold`: #b8860b (muted gilt)
- `--text`: #2a1f16 (sepia brown)
- `--muted`: #6a5a44

## Typography
- Headlines: Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond, or Cinzel — classical roman capitals, moderate tracking
- Body: Garamond or Sabon, 400 weight, 1.5 line-height
- Titles often centered above body text. Occasional small-caps subtitles.

## Composition
- Classical proportions: golden-ratio divisions, centered compositions, triangular focal hierarchies.
- Framed content blocks with subtle arched tops (like fresco panels).
- Imagery: Botticelli/Raphael-inspired figures, architectural arches, columns.
- Generous margins; content breathes like a page in an illuminated folio.

## Textures & details
- Fresco-like texture overlay at 8–12% opacity.
- Thin gilt borders (1–2px) in `--gold`.
- Laurel wreaths, acanthus leaf flourishes as corner ornaments.
- Small ornamental initial capitals.

## Avoid
- Modern sans-serifs, saturated digital color, grid-based layouts, photographic realism, drop shadows, neon.
