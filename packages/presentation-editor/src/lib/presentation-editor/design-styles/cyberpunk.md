## Palette
- `--bg`: #05060a (near-black)
- `--neon-cyan`: #00e5ff, `--neon-magenta`: #ff2a6d, `--neon-yellow`: #f9f871
- `--text`: #e0f7ff
- `--muted`: #5a6b80

## Typography
- Headlines: Orbitron, Rajdhani, or a wide techno display — uppercase, wide tracking (0.15em), glitch effects
- Body: IBM Plex Mono or JetBrains Mono, 400 weight
- Secondary labels in katakana-style or faux-Japanese accents (optional, subtle).

## Composition
- Neon-on-black base. Primary content in cyan/magenta with outer glow (`filter: drop-shadow`).
- HUD-style overlays: corner brackets, crosshairs, data readouts (TIME: 23:42, LAT: 34.05).
- Diagonal cuts and clipped corners on panels (clip-path).
- Scanline overlay using repeating linear gradient at 2px intervals, 6–10% opacity.

## Textures & details
- RGB chromatic aberration on headlines (offset cyan/magenta layers).
- Grid floor or wireframe perspective backgrounds.
- Glitch blocks: small rectangular displacements on text.
- Faint VHS noise overlay.

## Avoid
- Warm pastels, serif type, clean minimalism, natural photography, centered-balanced layouts, soft shadows.
