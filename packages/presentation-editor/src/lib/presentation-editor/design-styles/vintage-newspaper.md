## Palette
- `--bg`: #f4ecd8 (newsprint cream)
- `--text`: #1a1a1a (ink black)
- `--accent`: #7a1f1f (masthead red) — used sparingly
- `--muted`: #6a5a44 (faded ink)

## Typography
- Masthead: Old English Text (UnifrakturCook) or a heavy serif nameplate
- Headlines: a condensed slab or Didone — Playfair Display Black, Oswald Bold, or Bodoni Poster — tight leading, stacked decks
- Body: Source Serif Pro or Libre Caslon, 400 weight, justified, 1.35 line-height
- Headlines use decks: big head, smaller subhead under it, byline in italic small caps.

## Composition
- Multi-column broadsheet grid (2–4 columns) with thin vertical rules between columns.
- Masthead bar at top with date, edition, price. Thick double rule beneath.
- Headlines span multiple columns; body text wraps.
- Drop caps start long articles. "Continued on page X" teasers.

## Textures & details
- Paper grain, coffee-stain blotches, yellowed edges.
- Halftone dot pattern on any photograph.
- Registration marks, page numbers in the margins.

## Avoid
- Sans-serif body, saturated color, modern gradients, clean whitespace-heavy layouts, rounded corners.
